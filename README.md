# 📊 Excel Sales & Finance Analytics

An end-to-end Excel analytics project that turns raw sales and finance data into clear, decision-ready reports. It covers customer performance, market vs. target analysis, and Profit & Loss (P&L) statements, built with Power Query, Power Pivot, and DAX.

> **Messy data in. Clear decisions out.**

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

| Area | What I used |
|---|---|
| Data preparation | Power Query (ETL: Extract, Transform, Load) |
| Data modeling | Power Pivot, table relationships |
| Calculations | DAX (calculated columns) |
| Reporting | Excel, PivotTables, PDF report exports |
| Date intelligence | Custom date table, fiscal months and quarters |

---

## 📈 Part 1: Sales Report

### Objectives
1. Create a [Customer Performance Report](./Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf)
2. Compare [market performance against sales targets](./Customer%20Performance%20Report.pdf)

### Why it matters
- **Purpose:** Helps businesses monitor and evaluate their sales activities and performance.
- **Importance:** Reveals sales patterns and tracks key performance indicators (KPIs).
- **Role of the report:** Supports decisions on effective customer discounts, helps in negotiations with customers, and identifies expansion opportunities in promising countries.

---

## 💰 Part 2: Finance Report

### Objectives
1. Create P&L statements by [Fiscal Year](./P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Fiscal%20Year.pdf) and [Months](./P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Months.pdf)
2. Create P&L statements by [Markets](./P%26L%20Statement%20by%20Markets.pdf)

### Why it matters
- **Purpose:** Evaluates financial performance, supports decision-making, and improves communication with stakeholders.
- **Importance:** Enables benchmarking against industry peers and previous periods, and provides the foundation for budgeting and forecasting.
- **Role of the report:** Aligns financial planning with strategic goals and builds confidence in the organization's financial outlook.
